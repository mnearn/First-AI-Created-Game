MIRROR OF SELF

Five aspects. Five elements. One reflection.

You descend through five floors, each containing ten levels. Defeat enemies, allocate permanent stat points, claim randomized boss equipment, and confront a final Mirror that uses your own build against you.

The Mirror copies every ability you possess at 75% effectiveness and can wield every defeated boss's power — but it does not copy boss equipment.
